Research

Thesis · Nov 2023 – Feb 2024

Beta thalassemia trait in pregnant women

I measured how common beta thalassemia trait is among women visiting PIMS, Islamabad, and studied Hb, MCV, MCH, RBC count and HbA2 in carriers. The data can support low-cost early screening and prenatal counseling.
